Her back makes bending and lifting hard, so I’m building her a humanoid that does it for her: tidying, the dishwasher, wiping surfaces, laundry, fetch and carry, always under supervision. Cloud models decide what to do: Gemini Robotics ER 2 plans, a π model via the Pi API handles manipulation, Gemini 4 Argon builds simulations and data. On the robot, a policy runner on Jetson AGX Thor passes every action through a safety layer to the Unitree G1’s whole-body control and Dex3-1 tactile hands; if the link drops it falls back to GR00T N1.7 or π0.5, or stops safely. A training loop of teleoperated demos, fine-tuning, and evaluation in Isaac Lab and MuJoCo improves it. I promote a skill only at 80% success over 50 trials, with zero safety-stop violations.">
